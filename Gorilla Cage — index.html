GORILLA CAGE

You are the Gorilla. Entrepreneurs enter the cage with a pitch, a plan and a price. Read the numbers, do the math, take the deal — or send them home.

How to Play

  1. You start with ₹50,00,000 of fictional capital. 12 entrepreneurs will pitch to you, one at a time, in random order.
  2. Before every decision, a Math Check appears — a short CBSE Class 9 question based on that pitch. Solve it to unlock the Invest / Negotiate / Reject buttons. Correct answers earn bonus points; wrong answers lose a few, but you can retry with hints.
  3. Invest: pay the asked amount for the offered equity. Negotiate: counter with your own amount and equity — the founder has hidden limits. Reject: walk away.
  4. Each pitch has a success probability. A coin is weighted by that probability to decide whether the business thrives or struggles — but your decision score rewards evidence-based thinking, not luck.
  5. Game over after 12 pitches: see net worth, best and worst deals, and your two scores. High scores are saved on this device.

Reading a Pitch

  • Turnover is annual revenue; Expenses are annual costs; profit is their difference.
  • Profit margin = Profit ÷ Turnover × 100. Above ~12% is healthy for most fictional businesses here.
  • Implied valuation = Investment ÷ (Equity ÷ 100). Compare it to the business's realistic value — overpaying for equity costs you points and money.
  • Payback period = Investment ÷ your share of annual profit. Shorter is safer.

All businesses, founders and figures are fictional. Growth figures are estimates, not guarantees.
